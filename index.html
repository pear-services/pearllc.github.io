Products

Two ways we help schools work with the numbers that matter to them.

E-Rate Scout

Free for everyone

Search and follow the federal E-Rate program's public data. Look up schools, libraries, service providers and consultants, read Form 470 and 471 filings, and build your own queries over USAC Open Data.

  • Monitor an entity, a filing or a saved query and get an email when it changes
  • Bookmark records and come back to them from one dashboard
  • Export query results for your own analysis
Visit eratescout.com →

Edulytical

Built for one school at a time

Custom dashboards built from your school's own data. You share what you have — exports, spreadsheets or system access — and we build the dashboards and automations around your goals.

  • Attendance, assessments, enrollment and funding in one place
  • This year against last, and every target with how close you are
  • Clear numbers your whole team can read at a glance
Visit edulytical.app → Try the demo
